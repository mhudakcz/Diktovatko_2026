Changelog

What was added to Diktovátko and when, newest first. The app updates itself and shows you the changes before it does.

Back to homeGuideGitHub Releases

Version 0.12.2 24 September 2026 latest

  • The app language (Czech, English, German) can be switched right at the bottom left of the window and applies at once. In Settings, the language option is now at the top.
  • The website has a changelog: what's new on the home page and a separate page with every version.

Version 0.12.1 24 September 2026

  • The Cloud · fast / Local · slow switch is also on the left of the window, and in Settings it applies at once, no saving needed.
  • The website shows a clickable demo of the switch.

Version 0.12.0 24 September 2026

  • Before updating, you see what changed in every version since yours, even if you skipped a few.
  • The indicator is semi-transparent and can be dragged anywhere on the screen. Double-click puts it back at the bottom centre.

Version 0.11.1 24 September 2026

  • Fix: after switching Cloud / Local while recording, the app missed the hotkey release.
  • The indicator label has icons: a lightning bolt for fast, a snail for slow.

Version 0.11.0 24 September 2026

  • Switch between cloud and on-device transcription without deleting your key: click the label in the indicator, even while recording. Also in the icon menu and Settings.

Version 0.10.2 24 September 2026

  • The guide recommends turning on Zero Data Retention at Groq and explains more precisely what happens to your recording there.

Version 0.10.1 24 September 2026

  • The update check runs every 2 hours.

Version 0.10.0 24 September 2026

  • Automatic updates: Diktovátko notices a new version and updates itself with one click. Settings, key and history stay.

Version 0.9.1 24 September 2026

  • You can support the project via Ko-fi, on the website and in the icon menu.

Version 0.9.0 24 September 2026

  • Security audit: the Groq key lives in the system password store, the log contains no dictated text, text never lands in the wrong window, history can be deleted.
  • More reliable hotkeys and clear error messages.

Version 0.8.0 24 September 2026

  • The app, website and guide in Czech, English and German.

Version 0.7.0 24 September 2026

  • New website with a scroll-driven dictation demo. Version number in the app and a changelog.

Version 0.6.0 24 September 2026

  • Default hotkey Ctrl + Win, on Mac Ctrl + ⌘.

Version 0.5.0 24 September 2026

  • More detailed Mac guide: permissions step by step and troubleshooting.

Version 0.4.0 24 September 2026

  • Mac version and a beginner's guide, including how to create your own Groq key.

Version 0.3.0 24 September 2026

  • One window with history, statistics and settings. Time-saved statistics.

Version 0.2.0 24 September 2026

  • Project website on GitHub Pages.

Version 0.1.0 24 September 2026

  • First version: hold a key, speak, and the text appears at your cursor. Transcription on your computer or via Groq, local history.
