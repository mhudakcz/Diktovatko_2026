    Want text in a second? Get a free key.

    Without a key, Diktovátko transcribes on your computer. It works right away, but transcription takes about as long as the recording. With a free key from Groq, the text is ready in about a second.

    Offline on your computer
    10 s of speech, about 10 s wait
    With a Groq key
    10 s of speech, about 1 s wait
    1. Open console.groq.com/keys.
    2. Sign in with Google or email. No credit card needed.
    3. Click Create API Key, name it for example Diktovátko and confirm.
    4. Copy the key starting with gsk_ using Copy. It is shown only once.
    5. Click the Diktovátko icon, open Settings, paste the key into Groq API key and click Save settings. Done, your next dictation goes through Groq.

    The key is yours alone. Every user creates their own, it is stored only on your computer, and you shouldn't send it to anyone. If it leaks, delete it at console.groq.com and create a new one. The free tier covers hours of dictation a day, and without a card on file you are never charged.

    Offline or online. You decide.

    Diktovátko can transcribe in two ways. You switch between them simply by whether a Groq key is filled in under Settings.

    No key

    Offline on your computer

    • Your recording and text never leave your computer
    • Works without internet
    • Transcription takes about as long as the recording
    • About 1.6 GB downloads on first use
    With a Groq key

    Online via Groq

    • Text is ready in about a second
    • Light on your computer
    • Nothing is made public, Groq does not train on recordings, and with Zero Data Retention it keeps nothing
    • The recording of your voice leaves your computer
    • Needs an internet connection

    Heads up: in online mode, what you say leaves your computer

    With a key, the recording of your voice is sent to Groq servers in the USA and turned into text there. Don't dictate anything that must not leave your company this way: passwords, client personal data, health information or confidential contracts. For sensitive content, delete the key in Settings and transcription runs offline. Your dictation history is stored only on your computer in both modes.
